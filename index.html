forest_fires.csv

Modeling the Area Burned by Forest Fires

Forecasting wildfire damage in Portugal's northern region using linear regression.

Pythonscikit-learnMatplotlibNumPySeaborn
View repository →

The objective was to forecast the area burned by forest fires using historical environmental data, applying linear regression as the core predictive model.

The dataset went through preprocessing, data wrangling, and exploratory analysis to surface variables with strong correlation to the target and filter out major outliers.

The more interesting result wasn't the prediction itself: testing cast real doubt on linear regression as a reliable model for this kind of environmental data — even though the dataset's original authors had built their own linear model on it. The writeup digs into why.

Prometheus Children's School Score — Longitudinal & Predictive Analysis

Forecasting student academic performance through 2034 for an educational nonprofit, using linear regression.

PythonPostgreSQLstatsmodelspandaspsycopg2

Team project (6 analysts) · Stakeholder: Prometheus

Built for the Prometheus, a nonprofit partnering with a children's centre in rural Kenya to educate orphaned and vulnerable children. The center needed to understand long-term academic trends across its schools to guide programming and donor reporting.

I helped pull and query 5,363 exam records from PostgreSQL, normalize subject scores with a complex customized python function, and build the derived average_score metric used across the analysis. The longitudinal view (2010–2018) showed an overall downward trend in performance, with female students outperforming in Reading and Language but declining faster than male students over time.

The predictive stage used linear regression to forecast scores out to 2034 across gender, fee-payment status, and family size. Subject-count features carried the most predictive power. The forecast projects a continued decline for both genders — boys settling near 63.3 and girls falling below 54.3 by 2034 — a finding the team flagged as a call for earlier, targeted intervention rather than a prediction to accept at face value.
